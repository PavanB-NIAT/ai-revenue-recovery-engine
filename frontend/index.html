Portfolio Recovery Allocation (Version B)

Supervisory Economic Layer • Order-Independent Execution
"When recovery capacity is limited, the system doesn't just decide how to recover a payment — it decides which recovery opportunity deserves the next unit of recovery capacity."

Recovery capacity is finite (SMS/WhatsApp messaging limits, bank rate limits, merchant budget). The allocator prioritizes recovery opportunities by Expected Economic Value (EV) descending instead of processing eligible payments in natural arrival order (FIFO).

Engine Safety Guardrails & Invariants

Automated Circuit Breakers Protecting Merchant Margin and Customer Experience
• Terminal Security Circuit Breaker

Immediately blocks automated recovery for terminal declines (stolen cards, fraud flags, account frozen). Incurs 0 network attempts and zero financial liability.

• Dynamic Economic EV Bound

Evaluates EV = Amount × Confidence − Cost − Risk. Suppresses micro-tickets where message/routing fees exceed expected return.

• Bounded Multi-Hop Bound (MAX = 2)

Enforces a hard ceiling of 2 recovery attempts per transaction lifecycle. Completely eliminates infinite retry loops and network storms.

• Duplicate Strategy Prevention

If a strategy fails in Hop 1 (e.g. 3DS auth drop), that exact strategy is strictly excluded from candidate generation during Hop 2 state reassessment.

• Degraded Rail Circuit Breaker

Monitors real-time bank CBS health and secondary PG health. Blocks retry attempts against offline infrastructure, preventing bank bounce penalties.
